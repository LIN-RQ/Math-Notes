About

This website collects selected mathematical and numerical notes. The material focuses on partial differential equations, fluid mechanics, streamfunction-vorticity formulations, adjoint methods, and numerical schemes for steady-state problems.

The notes are intended as working documents: they may be revised, expanded, or reorganized as the underlying research progresses.

Repository Structure

The downloadable material is maintained in the associated notes repository. File paths in this webpage are relative paths, for example:

Math-Physics-Notes/numerical-methods/steady-navier-stokes-scheme.pdf

When you add a new PDF, place it in the appropriate folder and add a corresponding card or link in this index.html.
